The Gallery

A walkable 3D museum, in VR or a browser, that adds a new room for every few photos you upload.

WASD or arrows to walk · Shift to run · drag to look · click a photo to view it · click the map to jump rooms · VR: left stick walks, right stick turns, trigger opens a photo · photos stay on your device

Walk together

Everyone needs to be on the same wifi. One person hosts and the others join. Photos you add are shared with everyone, so you all see the same museum.
